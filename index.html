Questions

Good to know.

What does "single ingredient" mean?

Exactly what it sounds like. Each bag contains one ingredient, the one named on the front. No fillers, binders, flavors or preservatives.

What is freeze-drying?

Freeze-drying removes water from food by freezing it and then gently turning the ice to vapor under vacuum. Because it avoids high heat, more of the food's natural flavor and nutrition is preserved, and it stays shelf-stable without added preservatives.

Are the treats safe for cats too?

Yes. Our treats are made for all pets. Pure animal protein is a natural fit for cats, and most of our recipes are labeled for both dogs and cats.

How should I store them?

Keep the bag sealed in a cool, dry place. No refrigeration needed. Once opened, press the seal closed after each use to keep treats crisp.

How many treats can my pet have?

Treats should make up no more than about 10% of your pet's daily calories. Check the feeding guide on the bag, and talk to your vet if your pet has special dietary needs.
