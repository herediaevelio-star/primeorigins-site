Questions

Good to know.

What does "single ingredient" mean?

Exactly what it sounds like. Each bag contains one ingredient, the one named on the front. Our Beef Liver is 100% freeze-dried beef liver: no fillers, binders, flavors or preservatives.

What is freeze-drying?

Freeze-drying removes water from food by freezing it and then gently turning the ice to vapor under vacuum. Because it avoids high heat, more of the food's natural flavor and nutrition is preserved, and it stays shelf-stable without added preservatives.

Are the treats safe for cats too?

Yes. Our treats are made for dogs and cats at all life stages. For cats, we suggest 1–3 pieces daily.

How many treats can my pet have?

Small dogs (5–25 lbs) 2–4 pieces daily, medium dogs (26–50 lbs) 4–6, large dogs (51+ lbs) 6–8, and cats 1–3. Adjust for your pet's size, age and activity level, always supervise feeding and provide fresh water. These are intended for intermittent or supplemental feeding.

How should I store them?

Store in a cool, dry place and reseal the bag after opening to maintain freshness. No refrigeration needed.

Where are Prime Origins treats made?

Prime Origins treats are naturally produced by World Fresh Imports Inc. and are a product of the Dominican Republic.
